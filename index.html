The work

Defending Tree Ensembles

Gradient-boosted trees and random forests are everywhere in intrusion detection — they are fast, and they are interpretable. They are also evadable: perturb a malicious input just enough and it crosses the decision boundary unnoticed.

My research asks what to do about that. The short answer from the licentiate thesis: not adversarial retraining. Proactive defences are ineffective for tree ensembles and can make them more vulnerable. Leave the model alone and put a fast detector in front of it instead.

Built

Iceman · Maverick · VoTE

  • Iceman — counterexample-region evasion detection. >98% accuracy, 5–115× lower latency than the prior state of the art, with quaternary annotations for alert triage.
  • Maverick — autoencoder-based detection for automotive IDSs. Same accuracy as OC-Score, 85–563× faster — fast enough for a CAN bus.
  • VoTE extension — formal verification of tree ensembles under composite geometric perturbations, not just L-norm balls.

Also: transformer-based SIGMA rule evasion detection and attribution — several recommended fixes accepted upstream into the SIGMA repository.

2025 Licentiate thesis

Hardening Tree Ensembles: Real-Time and Effective Evasion Defences Beyond Adversarial Re-Training

Valency Oscar Colaco · Linköping University

This licentiate thesis shows that proactive defences such as adversarial retraining are ineffective for tree ensembles and can inadvertently make them more vulnerable to evasion attacks. Along these lines, it introduces Iceman and Maverick - two reactive prototype systems that advance the state of the art in evasion detection performance and speed, without modifying the underlying tree ensemble.

2023 Workshop paper

Formal Verification of Tree Ensembles against Real-World Composite Geometric Perturbations

Valency Oscar Colaco · Simin Nadjm-Tehrani

Extends VoTE to verify tree ensembles against composite geometric perturbations (affine transformations and pixel-wise lighting changes) by introducing a new abstraction-function-based robustness property checker. Shows that targeted data augmentation fails to improve robustness and can even reduce it.
